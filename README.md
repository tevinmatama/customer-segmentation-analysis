 Customer Segmentation Analysis

Project Overview
This project analyzes customer demographics, annual income, and spending scores to identify customer groups and support business marketing decisions.

Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

 Dataset
The dataset contains 200 customers and five columns, including age, gender, annual income, and spending score.

Key Findings
- **Total customers:** 200
- **Average age:** 38.85 years
- **Average annual income:** 60.56 (in thousands of dollars)
- **Average spending score:** 50.20 out of 100
- **Largest segment:** Average or Mixed, with 80 customers
- **High Income, High Spending:** 38 customers
- **High Income, Low Spending:** 37 customers

Visualizations
The project includes:
- Average spending score by gender
- Annual income versus spending score
- Number of customers by segment

 Business Recommendations
- Test loyalty rewards for high-income, high-spending customers.
- Explore targeted promotions for high-income, low-spending customers.
- Test affordable offers for relevant customer groups.
- Analyze the mixed segment further before making marketing decisions.

Method
Customers were assigned to five groups using simple income and spending-score thresholds. These are exploratory, rule-based segments rather than machine-learning clusters.

 Limitations
The dataset is limited to 200 records. The analysis describes patterns in this sample and does not establish causation.
